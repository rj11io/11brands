ground every other brand is a variation on.

## Notes

The signal is the dark-mode `--primary` from the blog's own `globals.css`. A
light-mode sibling of this brand must not reuse it — it measures 2.06:1 on a
light ground. See `ai-rj11io`, which is that sibling and uses the light-mode
token instead.

Contrast of the signal on its ground: **9.20:1**.
