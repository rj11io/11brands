# www.rj11.io

The main site. The only brand whose ground and ink are warm rather than neutral,
which is what makes it read as its own thing and not the blog with a different
accent.

**Domain:** www.rj11.io
**Mode:** dark
**Signal:** `#F97316`
**Ground:** `#0C0907`
**Ink:** `#FAF8F6`

## Notes

Ground and ink are overrides. Everything else in the family uses the plain dark
or light palette; this one is deliberately warmer on both, and the two overrides
above are the whole difference.

Contrast of the signal on its ground: **7.08:1**.
