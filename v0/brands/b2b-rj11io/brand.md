# b2b.rj11.io

The business-facing site. Gold on the standard dark ground, so it sits in the
family alongside the blog and intel without borrowing either of their colours.

**Domain:** b2b.rj11.io
**Mode:** dark
**Signal:** `#FBBF24`

## Notes

Amber 400. Gold on a dark ground has room to spare, so the choice was driven by
separation rather than contrast: the family already has `#F97316` orange on
`www-rj11io`, and gold sits next to orange on the wheel. Amber 400 is the step
that stays clearly yellow at 16 pixels instead of collapsing toward that orange
— amber 500 `#F59E0B` is closer to it and buys nothing, since both clear the
floor several times over.

Paired with `cc-rj11io`, which is the same gold idea on a light ground and
therefore a much darker hex.

Contrast of the signal on its ground: **11.86:1**.
