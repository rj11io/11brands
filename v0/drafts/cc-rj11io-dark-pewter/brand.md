# cc.rj11.io — dark pewter (rejected)

An archived attempt at `cc.rj11.io`, kept for reference. Not promoted. The
registered brand is `../../brands/cc-rj11io/`, which uses bright titanium
`#B4BDC4`.

**Domain:** cc.rj11.io
**Mode:** dark
**Signal:** `#64748B`

## Why it was rejected

Too dark. It reads as a deep grey rather than as silver, and the brief was
silver, so it lost on the one thing it had to get right.

That was a deliberate trade at the time, made for a reason worth keeping:

| Gap | This attempt | The titanium that replaced it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| vs ground `#0A0A0A` | 4.16:1 | 10.39:1 |
| vs ink `#FAFAFA` | **4.56:1** | 1.83:1 |
| vs footer `#A1A1A1` | 1.84:1 | 1.36:1 |

Slate 500 was the only step on the ramp that cleared all three gaps at once.
One step lighter (`#94A3B8`) sat at 1.01:1 against the footer grey. One step
darker (`#475569`) dropped to 2.61:1 against the ground and failed the 3:1 floor
for a non-text graphic.

## What it is still useful for

It is the strongest separation from the numeral any `cc.rj11.io` candidate
achieved, at 4.56:1 against the ink. If the mark ever needs the signal square to
read as a contrasting accent rather than a tonal highlight, this is the value that
does it, and the reason the brighter titanium cannot.

The trade the promoted brand accepted instead is recorded in
`../../brands/cc-rj11io/brand.md`: at 1.83:1 the square is a soft tonal step off
the numeral, so the mark reads as monochrome with a highlight. That is the
intended look, and this file is what it was chosen over.
