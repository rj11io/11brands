# ai.rj11.io

The AI sub-brand, and the first light one. Same green family as the blog, at the
shade a light ground actually needs.

**Domain:** ai.rj11.io
**Mode:** light
**Signal:** `#007A55`

## Notes

This brand shipped for a while carrying `#2BC88F`, the blog's dark-mode green,
on a near-white ground. That measures **2.06:1**, under the 3:1 a non-text
graphic needs. The fix was already written down elsewhere: the blog's
`globals.css` defines a light-mode `--primary`, `oklch(0.508 0.118 165.612)`,
which is `#007A55`. Reach for the existing token before inventing a shade.

Contrast of the signal on its ground: **5.14:1**.
