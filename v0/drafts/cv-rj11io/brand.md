# cv.rj11.io

The CV site. Light, blue.

**Domain:** cv.rj11.io
**Mode:** light
**Signal:** `#2563EB`

## Notes

Blue 600, not the blue 500 that would sit alongside the orange and red used on
the dark brands. Those signals are tuned to be bright against black and are
therefore weak against white: blue 500 measures only 3.52:1 here, clearing the
3:1 minimum by so little that any later tweak to the ground breaks it. 600 also
clears the stricter 4.5:1 that text needs, so the colour stays usable if it is
ever set in type.

Contrast of the signal on its ground: **4.95:1**.
