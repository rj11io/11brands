# blog.rj11.io

Blog-hosted content in the palette of `11archive`. The masthead stays
blog.rj11.io while the colours identify the leading brand.

## Palette decision

Mode light. Signal `#0F766E` on ground `#FAFAFA`: **5.24:1**.

Colours are `11archive`'s teal 700, unmodified — see that brand.md for the
ten-variant round and for the 11ai proximity note, which this brand inherits in
full. This key exists because content cards carry their consuming domain:
generating against `11archive` directly would put archive.rj11.io on a
blog.rj11.io asset.

## Notes

Text is 11blog's; colours are not. Favicon packs are byte-identical to
`11archive`'s, so this brand usually only needs og-content.

Both this brand and `11blog-11ai` are light-mode green-family palettes on the
same blog masthead, separated by hue alone (CIEDE2000 10.3, equal lightness).
Two blog cards side by side is the one place they are most likely to be seen
together.
