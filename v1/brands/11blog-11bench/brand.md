# blog.rj11.io

Blog-hosted content in the palette of `11bench`. The masthead stays blog.rj11.io while the colours identify the leading brand.

## Palette decision

Mode dark. Signal `#22D3EE` on ground `#0A0A0A`: **10.96:1**.

Colours are `11bench`'s cyan 400, unmodified — see that brand.md for the
selection round. This key exists because content cards carry their consuming
domain: generating against `11bench` directly would put bench.rj11.io on a
blog.rj11.io asset.

## Notes

Text is 11blog's; colours are not. Favicon packs are byte-identical to `11bench`'s, so this brand usually only needs og-content.
