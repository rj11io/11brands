# www.rj11.io

The main site. The only brand with a warm ground and ink.

## Palette decision

Mode dark. Signal `#F97316` on ground `#0C0907`: **7.08:1**.

Orange `#F97316` on warm ground `#0C0907` / ink `#FAF8F6`, carried from v0 `www-rj11io`. The two overrides are the entire difference from the standard dark palette.

## Notes

Known quirk carried from v0: the footer grey `#A1A1A1` is the family's cool neutral and is not a true blend of this brand's warm ground and ink, so anti-aliased text pixels sit slightly outside the brand's own colour triangle. Recorded in v0's verify skill; fix is a warm footer (`#A29F9D`) if ever wanted.
