# www.rj11.io

Test variant for 11io — the same orange 600 that `11labs-light-bright` carries, on 11io's own warm dark ground. If 11labs lands on this orange, the pair would share one hue across the two modes.

## Palette decision

Mode dark. Signal `#EA580C` on ground `#0C0907`: **5.58:1**.

Orange 600 `#EA580C` at 5.58:1 — comfortably clear of both the 3:1 floor and
the 4.5:1 aim. The interesting comparison is against the incumbent: 11io's
orange 500 `#F97316` measures 7.08:1 and reads hotter; 600 is deeper and calmer.
The same hex is floor-only (3.41:1) on 11labs' light ground, so if both brands
adopt it, the light side carries the risk, not this one.

## Notes

Keeps 11io's warm ground `#0C0907` and ink `#FAF8F6` — the warmth is the
brand's identity, so a variant that dropped it would be testing two changes at
once. Also inherits 11io's known quirk: the cool footer grey `#A1A1A1` off the
warm ground-ink line. Compare against `11io` directly; archive after the
decision either way.
