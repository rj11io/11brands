# cc.rj11.io

Bright titanium. The only neutral signal in the family.

## Palette decision

Mode dark. Signal `#B4BDC4` on ground `#0A0A0A`: **10.39:1**.

Titanium `#B4BDC4`, carried from v0 `cc-rj11io`. A neutral has no hue to separate it, so it was checked against three gaps: ground 10.39:1, ink 1.83:1, footer 1.36:1. The low ink figure is deliberate — the mark reads as monochrome with a highlight. Two rejected attempts (light bronze `#B45309`, dark pewter `#64748B`) are recorded in v0/drafts/.

## Notes

Verify note: a neutral signal collapses the gamut triangle to a sliver; use the forward (enumeration) test, never an inverse coefficient test.
