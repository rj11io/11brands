# bench.rj11.io

Test variant for 11bench (bench.rj11.io) — four candidates across the dark-mode blue arc, indigo to cyan. Dark blue is the family's free territory: 11cv's blue 600 lives on a light ground.

## Palette decision

Mode dark. Signal `#22D3EE` on ground `#0A0A0A`: **10.96:1**.

Cyan 400. The far edge, strongest contrast at 10.96:1.

## Notes

Closest of the four to 11blog's green-teal `#2BC88F` at 16 pixels — put the two favicons side by side before choosing this one; the family already lost a colour once to hues that collapse at that size. Blue 500 `#3B82F6` (5.38:1) was considered and dropped from the round: it tests depth, not hue, and the informative axis here is hue. Once a winner is picked, promote it to the plain `11bench` key and archive the rest.
