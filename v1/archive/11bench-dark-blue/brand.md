# bench.rj11.io

Test variant for 11bench (bench.rj11.io) — four candidates across the dark-mode blue arc, indigo to cyan. Dark blue is the family's free territory: 11cv's blue 600 lives on a light ground.

## Palette decision

Mode dark. Signal `#60A5FA` on ground `#0A0A0A`: **7.79:1**.

Blue 400. The centre of the arc — the colour most people mean by blue. Deliberately one hue step from 11cv's light-mode blue 600, but the two never share a ground.
